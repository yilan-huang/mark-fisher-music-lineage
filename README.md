# Electronic Music Through Fisher's Ear

A single-page interactive guide to the electronic music lineage that Mark Fisher writes about — from dub and Krautrock through rave and jungle to hauntology and the unfinished project of Acid Communism.

Built as a companion for reading Fisher's *Ghosts of My Life*, *Capitalist Realism*, *The Weird and the Eerie*, and the K-Punk blog.

## What's in it

- **19 genre cards** — each with sound description, key figures, and Fisher's specific theoretical engagement
- **D-Generation prelude** — Fisher's own early-90s music project, the embryo of K-Punk
- **Acid Communism coda** — the unfinished turn from diagnosis to recovery
- **Three-dimension tagging system** — how Fisher relates to each genre, its political valence, and key Fisherian concepts
- **Filter system** — filter genres by Fisher's relationship or political direction
- **YouTube links** — every recommended track and album linked for immediate listening
- **Collapsible sections** — scan the overview or expand for depth
